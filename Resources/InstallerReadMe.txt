Along 0.7.1
Your windows, in one scrolling row.

INSTALL
1. Drag Along into Applications.
2. Eject this disk image, then open Along from Applications.
3. Enable Accessibility access when Along asks.
4. Close setup and press Control + Option + P to start your row.

REQUIREMENTS
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later.

ABOUT THIS DOWNLOAD
This is a free, open-source preview build. It is ad-hoc signed, not
Developer ID verified or notarized by Apple. macOS may block first launch.
If blocked: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, then
choose Open Anyway for Along if you trust the download. Authenticate and
confirm Open. If Open Anyway is missing, try opening Along again first.
Follow Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/102445
Managed Macs may prohibit opening unverified apps. Do not disable Gatekeeper.

SHORTCUTS
Control + Option + Left / Right   Previous / next window
Control + Option + R              Cycle the selected window's width
Control + Option + P              Start / stop and restore windows
Control + Option + horizontal scroll   Move along the row

Stop & restore windows or Quit Along from the menu bar to restore your
original window positions. Force-quitting cannot restore them.

ACCESSIBILITY
In Privacy & Security, enable Along under Accessibility (called Device
Control and Data Access on newer macOS). Grant access to the installed
copy in Applications. If an update leaves access denied, remove the old
entry with the minus button, add the installed Along with plus, and enable it.

UPDATING
Quit Along or the older TileMe from its menu before installing an update.
Remove the old TileMe app and its Login Item when upgrading to Along.
Keep one installed copy. Ad-hoc signed updates may need a fresh Accessibility grant.

UNINSTALL
Quit Along from its menu, remove its Accessibility entry, and move
/Applications/Along.app to the Trash. Remove it from Login Items if added.
